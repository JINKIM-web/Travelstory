## Brand & Style

The design system is rooted in the poetry of physical travel journals, analog film warmth, and high-end editorial literature, translated into an ultra-fluid modern digital interface. It rejects cold, clinical tech minimalism in favor of a tactile, nostalgic warmth that treats user memories not as raw data, but as curated art.

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Evocative & Literary:** Invoking the sensation of heavy linen paper, sun-bleached postcards, and bespoke travel periodicals.
- **Intelligent & Subtle:** AI interventions feel like a sensitive editor or co-author refining memories rather than a robotic assistant.
- **Serene & Spacious:** Unhurried pacing, generous breathing space, and quiet dignity allow vivid film-toned photographs to command attention.

### Design Movement
**Warm Editorial Minimalism with Tactile Restraint.** The aesthetic balances the structure of independent travel magazines (such as *Cereal* or *Kinfolk*) with intuitive, buttery-smooth web interactions. Surfaces rely on organic linen tints, subtle warm keylines, and delicate ambient shadows rather than harsh divides.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from Mediterranean earth, baked terracotta tiles, coastal sage, and sunlit linen paper. Every hue is calibrated with warm undertones to eliminate artificial digital harshness.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#E06D53` - Terracotta Sunset):** Used deliberately for key focal actions, active progression indicators, and focal interactive elements. High-contrast, emotive, and human.
- **Secondary (`#7A8B7B` - Muted Olive / Coastal Sage):** Represents environmental stillness and geography. Applied to secondary tags, location coordinates, chronological paths, and organic status indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#D99B62` - Golden Amber):** Reserved for AI reflections, poetic insights, highlight moments, and warmth annotations.
- **Neutral Key (`#1E232A` - Deep Warm Slate):** Used for headlines and critical body text. Offers deep contrast without the stark artificial coldness of pure black (`#000000`).

### Surface & Border Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** `#FAF8F5` (Soft warm linen).
- **Surface Level 1 / Recessed:** `#F5F1EB` (Tonal containers, card gutters, secondary backdrops).
- **Surface Level 2 / Raised Card:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure pristine card surface, making photographs pop).
- **Border Subtle:** `#E9E4DC` (Delicate warm hairlines for structural integrity).
- **Text Secondary / Muted:** `#5E646E` (Balanced contrast for secondary editorial metadata).
- **Text Tertiary / Disabled:** `#8F949D` (Time stamps, photo metadata, captions).

## Typography

The type system pairs the humanistic cadence of classic Italian serif typography with the clinical clarity of high-performance grotesk sans-serif.

### Type Pairings & Rules
- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Evokes mid-century travelogues and modern arts journalism. Headings should be allowed generous vertical space. Use medium and semi-bold weights to avoid hairline dropouts on diverse display densities. Italic variants are reserved for AI introspections, temporal captions, and place subtitles.
- **Reading & Interface Body (`Inter`):** Selected for supreme neutral legibility across long narrative entries, data metadata, and step-by-step memory creation workflows. A loose body line-height (`1.6` to `1.7`) preserves reading comfort.
- **Editorial Sub-Caps:** All category indicators, chronological dates, and photo coordinate labels must utilize `label-caps` in uppercase with deliberate letter spacing (`0.08em`) to mimic physical publisher mastheads.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined, breathable 12-column layout designed to stage rich photography and deep prose side-by-side without visual tension.

### Canvas Grid Philosophy
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12 columns, max content container width `1200px` for storytelling journals, `1440px` for gallery canvas boards. Gutters set to `2.5rem` (`gutter-lg`) with page margins scaling up to `4rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, and `2.5rem` margins. Side-by-side photo and prose blocks automatically stack or adapt to a 5:3 ratio.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` outer margins. Creation flows switch to vertical uninterrupted cards.

### Rhythmic Principles
- Vertical rhythm favors generous margins (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`) between story chapters and AI memory compilations to provide an unhurried, contemplative pace.
- Memory cards and photo collages maintain a consistent `space-md` or `space-lg` gap, emphasizing masonry rhythms that honor both vertical portrait shots and horizontal landscapes.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical material cues rather than aggressive virtual drop-shadows. Depth is articulated through warm tonal layering, gentle diffused shadows with warm undertones, and micro-borders.

### Ambient Warm Elevation Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base):** Ground level `#FAF8F5`. No shadows.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers & Inset Panels):** `#F5F1EB` accompanied by a hairline border of `#E9E4DC` (1px solid). Used for prompt trays, secondary toolbars, and photo staging drops.
- **Tier 2 (Story & Memory Cards):** `#FFFFFF` surface lifted by an ultra-diffused, warm-tinted ambient shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(224, 109, 83, 0.03);`
  Bordered with a crisp 1px stroke in `#E9E4DC`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Controls & Navigation Modals):** `#FFFFFF` backed with high-surface frosted diffusion (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`):
  `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -8px rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.03);`
  Surrounded by 1px `#E9E4DC`.
- **Hover & Interaction Lift:** Interactive cards softly translate `-2px` on the Y-axis, transitioning shadow opacity from `0.04` to `0.08` over `300ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.

## Shapes

The geometry reflects soft, worn river stones and classic photographic print mounts. 

### Geometric Rules
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** Smooth `0.5rem` (`rounded-md` / `rounded-lg`) to `1rem` corner radii for human-scale touch points.
- **Cards & Story Panes:** Generous `1rem` to `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl`) corners to softly frame visual compositions and prevent severe edge cutoffs.
- **Photographic Media:** Handled with a consistent `0.75rem` or `1rem` radius. Never completely sharp unless intentionally styled in a vintage split-frame film montage.
- **AI & Highlight Pills:** Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) to visually telegraph dynamic, reactive states and playful tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in Terracotta Sunset (`#E06D53`) with white text (`#FFFFFF`). `0.75rem` padding vertical, `1.5rem` horizontal, radius `0.75rem` (`rounded-xl`). Subtle hover state shifts to `#CE5E45` with micro-lift.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with 1px border `#E9E4DC`, text in Slate (`#1E232A`). Hover transitions to `#F5F1EB`.
- **AI Action Button:** Subtle terracotta gradient or outline paired with tertiary accent glow, styled with `label-md` and accompanied by a custom sparkle icon.

### Chips & Badges
- **Location & Chronology Badges:** Background `#F5F1EB`, text `#7A8B7B`, border 1px `#E9E4DC`. Radius `9999px` (pill). Text uses `label-caps`.
- **Emotional Mood Tags:** Soft tinted pills (e.g., `#E06D53` at 8% opacity with `#E06D53` solid text) indicating emotional vibes (e.g., *Nostalgic*, *Golden Hour*, *Quiet Wanderlust*).

### Cards & Story Canvases
- **Memory Card:** White `#FFFFFF` background, `rounded-2xl`, 1px border `#E9E4DC`. 
  - Top: Aspect-ratio framed photograph (3:2 or 4:5) with soft `0.75rem` inner radius.
  - Middle: Date and Location badges in `label-caps`.
  - Body: Serif header (`headline-sm`) followed by `body-sm` excerpt.
  - Footer: AI-generated sensory keywords or atmospheric prompt tags.

### Emotional Quote Blocks (AI Insights)
- Left-bordered with a refined 3px solid `#E06D53` or `#D99B62`.
- Background set to translucent `#FAF8F5` or recessed `#F5F1EB` with `rounded-r-xl`.
- Typography styled using `quote-editorial` in italic `Playfair Display`, text `#1E232A`.
- Accompanied by a discreet AI signature tag ("Synthesized memory reflection") in `label-sm` muted slate.

### Creation Flow & Step Wizard
- Multi-step progress tracks rendered with soft terracotta filled dots connected by delicate hairlines (`#E9E4DC`).
- Full-width uncluttered form views with centered alignment, minimizing distractions to emulate writing in a private travel diary.

### Inputs & Text Areas
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E9E4DC`, corner radius `0.75rem`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#E06D53` with a warm glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(224, 109, 83, 0.15)`).
- Placeholder text in `#8F949D` set in `Inter`.
